Contaminated Site Remediation Experts

United Civil Group specialises in the remediation of contaminated sites across Queensland and New South Wales. We work closely with environmental consultants and regulatory bodies to deliver safe, compliant outcomes that return land to productive use.

Our experienced team has managed remediation projects ranging from small urban infill sites to large-scale industrial facilities. We understand the complexities of working with contaminated materials and maintain strict protocols to protect workers, the public, and the environment throughout every project.

Whether your site requires removal and off-site disposal of contaminated soil, in-situ treatment, or a combination of approaches, our team has the expertise and equipment to deliver the right solution for your project.

Our Remediation Capabilities

  • Contaminated soil excavation and disposal
  • Groundwater management and dewatering
  • Asbestos removal and disposal (Class A & B)
  • Site validation support and sampling
  • Environmental monitoring coordination
  • Soil treatment and stabilisation
  • Capping and containment systems
  • Regulatory liaison and compliance
Frequently Asked Questions

Site Remediation FAQ

We handle a wide range of contamination types including hydrocarbon-impacted soils, heavy metals, asbestos-containing materials, pesticides, and other industrial contaminants. Our team works closely with environmental consultants to develop site-specific remediation strategies.
Project timelines vary depending on the type and extent of contamination, site size, and regulatory requirements. Small-scale remediation may take a few weeks, while complex multi-phase projects can span several months. We provide detailed timelines during the planning phase.
We work closely with environmental consultants and regulatory bodies throughout the remediation process. While the environmental consultant typically manages the regulatory framework, we ensure all practical works comply with approved remediation action plans and relevant environmental legislation.
United Civil Group provides site remediation services throughout Queensland and New South Wales, including the Gold Coast, Brisbane, South East Queensland, and regional areas across both states.
