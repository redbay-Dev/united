Civil Infrastructure Experts

United Civil Group delivers comprehensive civil infrastructure construction services across Queensland and New South Wales. From new road construction to utility upgrades and public infrastructure works, our experienced team has the capability to deliver projects of all scales.

We work closely with local councils, state government agencies, and private clients to deliver infrastructure that meets exacting standards and serves communities for decades. Our team brings deep knowledge of council specifications, authority requirements, and best-practice construction methods.

With a modern fleet of GPS-equipped machinery and a skilled workforce, we deliver precise, efficient results whether the project involves a new residential street or a major trunk infrastructure upgrade.
