Complete Subdivision Development

United Civil Group delivers comprehensive subdivision civil works for both urban residential and industrial developments. From initial bulk earthworks through to final road sealing and landscaping, we manage every stage of the civil construction process.

Our experienced project management team coordinates all civil and utility components to ensure seamless integration, efficient delivery, and compliance with local council and authority requirements. We understand the importance of meeting development milestones and work proactively to keep your project on track.

With strong relationships across council authorities, utility providers, and industry stakeholders in Queensland and New South Wales, we streamline the approvals and construction process for land developers and builders.
