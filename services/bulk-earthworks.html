Bulk Earthworks Specialists

United Civil Group's extensive fleet of modern earthmoving equipment enables us to undertake bulk earthworks projects of any scale with efficiency and accuracy. From initial site clearing through to final trim and grading, our experienced operators deliver precision results using GPS machine control technology.

Whether your project requires tens of thousands or hundreds of thousands of cubic metres of material movement, our team has the experience and equipment to manage complex earthworks programs. We focus on optimising cut-to-fill ratios, minimising material haulage, and maintaining strict compaction standards throughout.

Our earthworks fleet includes excavators from 5 to 50 tonnes, GPS-fitted dozers, graders, compaction equipment, water carts, and a full complement of trucks for material haulage. All equipment is regularly maintained and operated by skilled, experienced operators.

Our Earthworks Capabilities

  • Site clearing and grubbing
  • Large-scale cut and fill operations
  • Detailed excavation
  • Compaction and proof rolling
  • Final trim and grading (GPS)
  • Benching and batter construction
  • Material import and export management
  • Dust suppression and environmental controls
