About Us

Company Overview

United Civil Group is a premier civil contracting company delivering comprehensive earthworks and civil construction services throughout Queensland and New South Wales. With our leadership team's proud history spanning more than three decades in the industry, we bring unmatched expertise and a wealth of practical knowledge to every project we undertake.
We have established ourselves as trusted partners for projects of all scales and complexities—from early works site preparation through to major civil infrastructure delivery. Our expertise extends across contaminated site remediation, industrial and urban subdivisions, civil infrastructure projects, and comprehensive civil utility services.
As an Australian-owned business, we understand the unique challenges of working across diverse Australian environments and maintain strong relationships with local councils, government bodies, and private sector clients alike.

Our Approach

At United Civil Group, we understand that every project represents unique challenges and opportunities. Our approach combines proven methodologies with innovative solutions, ensuring we deliver outcomes that exceed expectations whilst maintaining strict adherence to timelines and budgets. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients, consultants, and stakeholders through open communication and transparency. From the initial consultation through to project completion, we work collaboratively to understand your objectives and deliver tailored solutions that meet your specific requirements. Our experienced project management team ensures seamless coordination across all aspects of delivery, with regular progress reporting and proactive issue resolution to keep your project on track.

What We Do

Core Capabilities

Bulk Earthworks & Excavation

Large-scale cut and fill operations, site levelling, bulk material handling, and mass excavation for developments of all sizes.

Contaminated Site Remediation

Comprehensive remediation services including assessment, excavation, treatment, and validation of contaminated soils and groundwater.

Land Development & Subdivision

Complete subdivision delivery from initial earthworks through to final handover, including all civil infrastructure requirements.

Road Construction & Maintenance

Construction of new roads, carparks, and access ways, plus maintenance and rehabilitation of existing road infrastructure.

Drainage & Stormwater Systems

Design and construction of detention basins, gross pollutant traps, bioretention systems, and complete stormwater networks.

Concrete Works

Kerbing, footpaths, pavements, crossovers, and general concrete construction to council and RMS specifications.

Utility Installation

Water mains, sewer reticulation, electrical and communications conduits, with connections to existing network infrastructure.

Sewer Pump Stations & Rising Mains

Complete installation of pump stations and associated rising mains, including commissioning and testing.

Environmental & Erosion Control

Sediment control measures, erosion prevention, revegetation, and environmental protection throughout project delivery.
